# PRD: จักรพรรดิ/จักรพรรดิทองคำ (Group I) — Per-Position Diversity via Round-Robin Construction

## Problem Statement

The candidate diversity fix shipped in `PRD-imperial-formula-candidate-diversity.md` (commit `7ef4779`) measured the wrong dimension. It improved *average pairwise Hamming distance* across the whole 6-digit candidate set (1.80→2.53), but the user judges "does this look clustered" by a different, more visible signal: **how many distinct digits show up in each column (digit position) across the 10 candidates**. After the fix shipped, real output for I1 still showed only 2-3 distinct digits at half the positions (`[1,3,3,2,2,3]` out of a possible 10), and the user's own screenshot comparison — a real รางวัลที่ 4 result table, where every column is close to fully spread across 0-9 — made clear that "better than before" wasn't the bar; "looks like a real draw" was.

Separately, the natural next step of the round-1 mechanism (widen the per-position raw cap further, e.g. top-8, to give the greedy selector enough material to hit a per-position target) has a fatal performance problem: `_imperialCandidates`'s cartesian expansion is `K^6` before dedupe. Top-3 was 729; top-8 would be **262,144** combinations generated *every time the formula runs* — including once per draw inside the Backtest page's loop over hundreds of draws. That's not viable.

## Solution

Replace round-1's "generate a large candidate pool, then greedily select for diversity" approach entirely with **direct round-robin construction per digit position** — no cartesian pool, no `_imperialCandidates`, no `_imperialDiverseSelect`. For each of the 6 positions, `_digitPosFreq` already ranks all 10 possible digits (0-9) by frequency; take the top **K=8** of those 10 per position, and build each of the `topN=10` final candidates by picking, at each position, the digit at index `(i%K + floor(i/K)*(p+1)) % K` of that position's top-8 ranked list (`i` = candidate index 0-9, `p` = position index 0-5). This is O(topN × 6) regardless of K — no combinatorial blowup — and deterministically guarantees **exactly 8 distinct digits at every position** across the 10 candidates (proof: candidates 0-7 alone already use ranks 0-7 uniformly at every position, which are 8 distinct values by construction of the ranking; candidates 8-9 only reuse those same 8 ranked digits in a staggered order, never introducing rank-8/9, and never exactly reproducing an earlier row — verified by hand-derivation and confirmed no full-row collisions even on adversarial fixtures, see Testing Decisions).

จักรพรรดิทองคำ (I2)'s arithmetic blend is redefined from a *whole-6-digit-candidate* score (which required evaluating full combinations, incompatible with per-position construction) to a **per-position digit-level blend**: at each position, re-rank the 10 digits by `0.8 × (count / maxCountAtThatPosition) + 0.2 × ((9 - |digit − targetDigitAtThatPosition|) / 9)` instead of pure frequency, then apply the same round-robin construction on top of that re-ranked list. This keeps จักรพรรดิทองคำ meaningfully different from จักรพรรดิ (different per-position ranking → different top-8 sets → different round-robin output) while fitting the new architecture.

## User Stories

1. As a dashboard user, I want every digit position across จักรพรรดิ's and จักรพรรดิทองคำ's 10 candidates to show close to a full spread of distinct digits (target: 8 out of 10 possible), so that the output visually resembles a real draw's spread rather than a narrow cluster.
2. As the formula maintainer, I want the per-position distinct-digit guarantee to be deterministic (proven by construction), not probabilistic or dependent on a relaxation fallback, so that I don't need to re-verify "did it actually reach the target this time" on every run.
3. As the formula maintainer, I want candidate generation to stay O(topN × positions) regardless of how many digits per position are considered, so that widening the diversity target never risks a combinatorial performance blowup — this rules out any cartesian-expansion-based mechanism, including round-1's approach at K=8.
4. As the formula maintainer, I want candidate #1 (index 0) to always be the pure highest-frequency (or highest-blended, for จักรพรรดิทองคำ) digit at every position — i.e. exactly what round-1 and the original formula both already guaranteed — so that the "best single guess" is never sacrificed for the sake of spread.
5. As the formula maintainer, I want จักรพรรดิทองคำ's blend (frequency + arithmetic closeness) reinterpreted per-position rather than dropped, so that it remains a meaningfully different formula from จักรพรรดิ under the new architecture, not just a relabeled duplicate.
6. As the formula maintainer, I want `_imperialCandidates` and `_imperialDiverseSelect` (both introduced in the round-1 PRD) removed once nothing calls them, rather than left as dead code, since the new construction method supersedes them entirely.
7. As the formula maintainer, I want this change scoped to candidate construction only — no change to `_digitPosFreq`'s counting logic itself, `_buildPrize1to5Pool`, the `pool6`/`pool_tail3` backtest field types, or any other formula group.
8. As the formula maintainer, I want the resulting Edge re-measured against the real 455-draw dataset and reported honestly in `CHANGELOG.md`, exactly as round-1 did, regardless of direction.

## Implementation Decisions

- **New shared function `_imperialRoundRobin(rankedPerPosition, topN=10, K=8)`:**
  - `rankedPerPosition`: an array of 6 arrays, each the caller's own best-to-worst ordering of *all 10* digit strings for that position (จักรพรรดิ passes `_digitPosFreq`'s output as-is; จักรพรรดิทองคำ passes its own per-position blended re-ranking — see below).
  - For `i` in `0..topN-1`: let `lap = Math.floor(i / K)`, `r = i % K`. For each position `p` in `0..5`: `idx = (r + lap * (p + 1)) % K`; take `rankedPerPosition[p].slice(0, K)[idx]`. Concatenate across all 6 positions to form the candidate's 6-digit string.
  - No relaxation, no fallback, no dedupe pass needed — the formula is deterministic and collision-free by construction for `topN=10, K=8` (verified in Testing Decisions). If `topN <= K`, `lap` is always 0 and this degenerates to "each candidate `i` uses rank `i` at every position" (still valid, still collision-free, since ranks 0..topN-1 are already distinct per position).
- **จักรพรรดิ (`_imperialFormula`):** unchanged pool-building (`_buildPrize1to5Pool`, `_digitPosFreq`), then call `_imperialRoundRobin(ranked.map(posRanked => posRanked.map(e => e.digit)), topN, 8)` in place of the round-1 candidate-generation + diverse-select pipeline.
- **จักรพรรดิทองคำ (`_imperialGoldFormula`):** keep the existing `target` derivation (`(prize1 + nextDay×nextMonth×nextYear2) mod 1,000,000`, padded to 6 digits). For each position `p`, re-rank `ranked[p]`'s 10 digit entries by `0.8 × (count / ranked[p][0].count) + 0.2 × ((9 - |Number(digit) - Number(target[p])|) / 9)` descending (tie-break: original frequency count descending, then digit ascending) to get that position's own ordering, then call `_imperialRoundRobin` on the resulting per-position lists with the same `topN=10, K=8`.
- **Remove** `_imperialCandidates` and `_imperialDiverseSelect` (both from the round-1 PRD) once no call sites reference them — confirm via grep before deleting.
- **No change** to `_digitPosFreq`, `_buildPrize1to5Pool`, `pool6`/`pool_tail3` field types, `_formulaHitForField`, or any other formula group.
- **No change** to `topN` (stays 10) or to I1/I2's labeling/gating status (still out of scope, per the round-1 PRD's own deferral — not reopened here).

## Testing Decisions

- Rewrite the relevant sections of `scripts/test_imperial_formula.js` (existing fixture-script pattern) to match the new architecture:
  - Direct unit test of `_imperialRoundRobin`: given a synthetic `rankedPerPosition` (6 positions × 10 digits each, distinct order per position), verify (a) candidate 0 uses rank-0 at every position, (b) all `topN=10` returned candidates are unique strings, (c) each position shows exactly `K=8` distinct digits across the 10 candidates, (d) digits ranked 8-9 (the bottom 2) never appear in any candidate.
  - Adversarial fixture: all 6 positions share the *identical* rank ordering (the worst case for accidental row collisions, hand-verified during grilling to still produce 10 distinct rows) — confirm no duplicates and the K=8 per-position guarantee still holds.
  - `_imperialFormula` end-to-end: on a realistic pool fixture, verify per-position distinct-digit counts are exactly `[8,8,8,8,8,8]` (or fewer only if a position's underlying pool has fewer than 8 digits with nonzero count — should not happen since `_digitPosFreq` always ranks all 10 digits regardless of count).
  - `_imperialGoldFormula` end-to-end: verify its per-position ranking genuinely differs from จักรพรรดิ's pure-frequency ranking for a fixture where the arithmetic target meaningfully disagrees with frequency at some position (reuse/adapt the existing "genuinely disagree" fixture), and that its own per-position distinct-digit counts also hit `[8,8,8,8,8,8]`.
  - Confirm `_imperialCandidates` and `_imperialDiverseSelect` are no longer referenced anywhere in `formula-engine.js` after removal (simple string-absence check, mirroring the existing "no fable references remain" pattern in `scripts/test_pool_field_rename.js`).
- Regression run of all other fixture scripts (`test_codexpool_coeffs.js`, `test_codexpool_presets.js`, `test_pool_field_rename.js`, `test_decision_track_record.js`) — none should need changes.
- Re-run the real 455-draw backtest (same method as round-1: compare against the dataset via the running server's API) and record the resulting Edge for both I1 and I2 in `CHANGELOG.md` honestly, whatever it is.
- Manual/direct verification of the actual per-position digit spread on live data (same substitute method used in round-1 given the recurring port-8509 conflict with other sessions, if it recurs): print actual candidates and per-position distinct-digit counts for a real previous-draw row, confirm they hit `[8,8,8,8,8,8]` (or note any position that doesn't and why).

## Out of Scope

- Everything already deferred by the round-1 PRD remains deferred here: retroactive gating/experimental labeling for I1/I2, adding `pool_tail3` or other secondary metrics for I1/I2, referencing more than the immediately previous draw, any change to Groups A-H.
- Changing `topN` (stays 10) or `K` beyond 8 — 8 was chosen deliberately as "spread but not total" (see Further Notes); revisiting this value is a separate discussion if the result still doesn't look right.
- A formal validation/live-window split for accepting this change — same reasoning as round-1: this isn't an Edge-chasing change, it's a visual/structural correctness fix, independent of accuracy.
- Any attempt to make จักรพรรดิทองคำ's per-position blend produce results identical in *shape* to its old whole-candidate blend — the redefinition is a genuine reinterpretation necessitated by the architecture change, not a like-for-like port.

## Further Notes

- Why K=8 and not K=10 (full spread): discussed and agreed during grilling — at K=10, frequency (จักรพรรดิ's core signal) would stop influencing *which* digits appear at all (every digit would appear regardless of rank), reducing it to a pure ordering/tie-break role. K=8 still excludes the 2 least-informative digits per position, leaving frequency meaningfully load-bearing while getting most of the way to "looks like a real spread."
- Why round-robin instead of a smarter/weighted-random per-position sampler: round-robin is deterministic (no test flakiness, no need to seed/verify randomness), trivially fast, and provably hits the K=8 target exactly every time — a weighted-random sampler would need to be verified statistically rather than by construction, and could occasionally under-shoot K by chance.
- This is a direct architectural replacement of `PRD-imperial-formula-candidate-diversity.md`'s mechanism (that PRD's `_imperialCandidates`/`_imperialDiverseSelect` are removed by this one), filed separately per this project's convention of giving each round of decision-making its own record (see e.g. how FABLE's multiple tuning rounds each got their own CHANGELOG entry rather than rewriting history). That PRD's Problem Statement and reasoning remain valid context for *why* this work exists; this PRD documents why its specific mechanism didn't hold up and what replaced it.
- Hard rules from `DEVELOPMENT_PLAN.md` continue to apply unchanged.
- Recommended execution: Sonnet 5, medium-high effort. Reasoning: same file, moderate scope, but the round-robin collision-avoidance formula needs careful test coverage (including the adversarial identical-ranking fixture) to be trusted without re-deriving it by hand every time.
